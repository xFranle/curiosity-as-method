I am interested in understanding why we think the way we do. How we build our beliefs, what shapes our decisions, and why we so often accept explanations without stopping to question them.

This is a space where I explore those questions through psychology, philosophy, the behavioral sciences and neuroscience. I want to connect ideas, test them against research, and find new ways of interpreting what happens around us.

I am not looking for definitive answers. I write to develop ideas, put them to the test, and keep discovering new questions.
